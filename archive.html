Methodology

Each image scored (0-100%) across three dimensions: Visual Identity (40%), Behavioral Alignment (30%), Voice/Audience Alignment (30%). Images below 50% are contradictions.

About

This project sits at the intersection of sincerity and irony, treating personal identity as both brand and human experience. By scoring each image against my own guidelines, I reveal both the labor of self-presentation and its inevitable failures. The contradictions become the most honest documentation of all.
