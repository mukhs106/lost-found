Lost & Found: An Atlas of Being

An intimate, explorable archive of memory and identity

About

This is a personal archive that maps the landscape of memory through photographs, notes, and moments spanning 1990-2024. Each image is marked as either "lost" (distant, fragmented) or "found" (present, integrated), acknowledging that memory is about both absence and presence.

The archive organizes memories across eight emotional themes: notes, moments, faces, nostalgia, play, belonging, change, and limbo. These aren't rigid categories—many memories belong to multiple themes, creating overlapping constellations of meaning.

How to Navigate

Drift View: Wander through a field of scattered memories. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Memories cluster naturally into groups, with some wandering freely. Let momentum carry you.

Descent View: Look down into layered rings of memory. Zoom reveals deeper layers. Drag to rotate. All memories hold equal visual weight regardless of their depth.

Click any image to see it larger with its full context: title, year, themes, and written reflection.

Filters

Use filters as tools for discovery. Select themes to see overlaps. Adjust the timeline to explore specific periods. Quick Jump presets offer curated pathways through the collection. Experiment—the archive reveals different stories depending on how you look.

A Note

This is a living archive. Memories are added over time, not all at once. Some images are notes to self—click them to read the full reflection. Take your time. There's no correct path through.
